Obin Academy

A self-paced e-learning web application built for a company: students browse the company's courses, pay for a course, and learn through on-demand video lessons — a Udemy-style platform customized for one company's courses and students.

Overview

Obin Academy is a web application I built for a company to deliver its own courses online. It works like a Udemy-style platform, but customized for a single company: its course catalog, its students, its branding.

How it works

  • Browse — students explore the company's course catalog and course details.
  • Pay — students pay for the course they want, unlocking access.
  • Learn — lessons are delivered as on-demand videos, learned at the student's own pace.

Why it matters

Instead of sending students to a generic marketplace, the company keeps its courses, payments and learners inside its own platform — a focused learning experience built around its own content.
