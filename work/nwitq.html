Overview

NWITQ is a news intelligence platform that brings together coverage from multiple trusted Ugandan outlets and presents related reporting as one evolving story.

Problem

Following one story means jumping between websites, reading repeated headlines and piecing together the full picture yourself — what sources agree on, what only one reports, where they differ and how the story develops.

My role

Product design, React Native application and AI clustering pipeline.

Architecture & approach

Multi-source news aggregation with AI-powered story clustering (OpenAI) grouping coverage of the same events, with source attribution on everything, story timelines and a daily digest.

Engineering decisions

The goal is not to replace news organisations or decide which source is ‘right’. The product shows agreement, single-source reporting and differences side by side, with sources visible, and lets the reader judge.

Challenges

The core design challenge is clustering: recognising that differently-worded reports from different outlets describe the same event, and keeping that grouping honest as the story develops.

Outcome & lessons

MVP in progress. A waitlist is open and an example story is published — honest feedback, including that the idea is unnecessary, is welcome.
