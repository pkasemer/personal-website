PERSONAL FINANCE · MOBILE PRODUCT

InToday

A personal finance product focused on daily spending guidance, payday-aware budgeting and practical money management.

In developmentSwiftSwiftUIPythonFastAPISQLite

Overview

InToday is a personal finance application centred on one concept: Safe Today — how much you can safely spend today after bills, savings and commitments are accounted for.

Problem

Most budgeting fails on pacing, not totals: people feel rich on the 1st and broke by the 15th because nothing translates a monthly budget into a daily decision.

My role

Product concept, specification, iOS experience design and backend sync service.

Architecture & approach

Each plan runs from the 1st to month-end and is payday-aware: salary landing on the last business day of the previous month funds the next month’s plan. Locked savings can split across named goals; Safe Today depends on the total locked. Tracking is offline-first with backend synchronisation.

Engineering decisions

Logging an expense is deliberately three taps — Amount → Tag → Done — because a finance habit dies if logging is slow. Sync is treated as the hard part: stable IDs and idempotent writes, so a retried upload updates the same record instead of duplicating it.

Challenges

Keeping a spec as the single source of truth while the app is built screen by screen — when spec and build disagree, stop and decide rather than letting drift decide.

Outcome & lessons

In active development, built in public: product spec, app build and a live backend sync service. The mascot is Into the pangolin — sleek and unhurried, like the pace the app teaches.
