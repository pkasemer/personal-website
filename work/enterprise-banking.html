Enterprise Banking Systems

Engineering digital banking capabilities, enterprise integrations and API-driven solutions within a regulated financial services environment.

Professional experienceFinacleREST APIsC#Node.jsSQL ServerOracle

Overview

Digital banking and enterprise technology initiatives within financial services — integrating banking systems, developing digital channels and improving how customers access financial services.

Problem

Customers and staff depended on channels and systems that were not well connected: account opening, payments and support journeys needed to work end to end, with data arriving clean enough for backend systems to act on.

My role

Software development, systems integration, technical implementation, testing, troubleshooting and digital project delivery.

Architecture & approach

Banking API development and integration, core banking integration, digital account opening services, payment integrations and transaction processing, plus customer support and operational tooling. Structured customer journeys on WhatsApp use the WhatsApp Cloud API and WhatsApp Flows — proper form screens inside the conversation — so data is validated at the field level instead of parsed from free text.

Engineering decisions

Chat for conversation, Flows for anything with fields. Structured input over free text wherever a backend system has to consume the result. Everything is presented at an architectural and functional level only — no confidential data, payloads or internal infrastructure.

Challenges

Integrating systems with very different contracts — REST and SOAP services, a core banking platform and third-party channels — while keeping each journey reliable in production.

Outcome & lessons

The same structured-journey pattern now serves account opening, merchant payments and custom support. The lesson that repeats: an integration is only finished when its failures are visible and its data is trustworthy.
